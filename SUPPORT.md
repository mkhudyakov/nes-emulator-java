## Games supported

Mapper 0 / NROM (small, early titles, no bank-switching):
- Super Mario Bros.
- Donkey Kong, Donkey Kong Jr., Donkey Kong 3
- Mario Bros., Balloon Fight, Ice Climber, Excitebike
- Duck Hunt, Pinball, Tennis, Golf, Baseball, Clu Clu Land
- Lode Runner, Bomberman, Galaxian, Xevious

Mapper 4 / MMC3 (mid-to-late, more sophisticated titles):
- Super Mario Bros. 2 & Super Mario Bros. 3
- Mega Man 3, 4, 5, 6
- Kirby's Adventure
- TMNT II: The Arcade Game & TMNT III
- Double Dragon II & III
- Ninja Gaiden II & III
- Super C (Super Contra), Gauntlet II, Rad Racer II, Klax
